Every successful drain jointly requires all known identities to be absent, no
holder of the record inode, and empty qualified PGID/SID scopes. Exit status or
disappearance of the tracer alone is not considered sufficient proof. If an
observation is ambiguous, the harness does not widen the target: it waits for
the TTL, fails, and preserves the sandbox.

## Architectural decision

The only candidate v0 stop strategy is:

```text
proot-distro kill SESSION_PID
```

`SESSION_PID` is the exact identifier observed and persisted while the daemon
still retains the child handle and identity. It is not passed to `kill(2)`,
replaced with an alias, or widened with `--all`. Direct PGID TERM is
insufficient for a descendant that changes session; the engine can instead
find guests left alive after the tracer dies through inherited record holders.

The engine's grace period is fixed and best effort, so v0 does not expose
`stopGracePeriod`. After the real daemon is lost, even a positive registry row
remains complementary evidence and does not reconstruct ownership by itself:
daemon death, reaping, and reconciliation remain an S5 gate. S3 does not
qualify ownership or crashes during `install`; those remain S4.
